RECOMMENDATION 01

Organize your messaging around the buyer's stage, not just the UGC opportunity.

Instead of treating everyone interested in UGC as the same customer, create a clear progression. Someone who has never landed a deal needs a different solution from someone already working with brands but struggling to build predictable income.

  • Give each audience its own headline and core promise.
  • Match the VSL, examples, and objections to the audience entering that funnel.
  • Direct each audience toward the offer and next step that best fits their current stage.

Turn the VSL into your pre-application sales experience.

Move the VSL ahead of the application. Use it to establish the problem, introduce your approach, explain the transformation, and show why your offer is worth considering. By the time someone reaches the application, they should already understand the opportunity and have a reason to take the next step.
