OBSERVATION 01

Thank You Page Broken After Scheduling Call

After a prospect schedules a call, the page redirects them to a GoDaddy domain-for-sale page instead of a proper confirmation experience. Right after someone commits time to speak with you, the journey suddenly feels broken and unprofessional.

  • The prospect gets no clear confirmation that the booking was successful.
  • The broken redirect can create doubt about whether the call is actually scheduled.
  • The business loses a valuable opportunity to reinforce the decision and set expectations.

RECOMMENDATION 01

Turn the booking confirmation into the next step toward the sale

Create a dedicated post-booking experience that not only confirms the call but also gets prospects looking forward for the conversation.

  • Confirm the booking and clearly communicate what happens next.
  • Set expectations for the call and what the prospect should prepare.
  • Reinforce the value of showing up and having the conversation.
0615
OBSERVATION 02

Every lead enters the same sales process, regardless of fit.

The current process doesn't gather enough information to distinguish between prospects who are ready to scale and those who lack the client base, capacity, or marketing foundation to benefit from the offer. As a result, the business has limited insight into lead quality before investing time in a call.

  • Sales time can be spent on prospects who aren't a strong fit.
  • Calls begin without a clear picture of the prospect's needs or constraints.
  • The business has less information to prioritize the strongest opportunities.

RECOMMENDATION 02

Build a qualification system around business readiness

Introduce a short, focused application that helps identify fit and gives the sales process better information before each call.

  • Qualify prospects based on their current business stage and goals.
  • Assess their ability to fulfill more client work and implement a growth strategy.
  • Use the responses to prioritize calls and prepare a more relevant sales conversation.
0815
OBSERVATION 03

The emails remind prospects to attend, but don't build commitment.

The email sequence focuses on reminding prospects about their scheduled call, rather than preparing them for it or strengthening their desire to attend. The communication handles the logistics, but misses the opportunity to build confidence in the decision they already made.

  • Prospects receive reminders without much additional reason to value the call.
  • The perceived importance of the conversation isn't actively reinforced.
  • The sequence relies on the original booking intent to carry the prospect through to attendance.
