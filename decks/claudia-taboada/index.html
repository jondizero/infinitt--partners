Your current offer model will undoubtedly cap your future growth

Your offer is structured around your personal availability. As enrollment grows, so does the number of private sessions you need to deliver. That means the very thing that should create business growth—more clients—can quickly become the reason you have less time and flexibility.

  • You're building a business that depends heavily on your continued personal involvement.

RECOMMENDATION 01

Separate the transformation from the level of access clients receive.

Build a core program that delivers the main outcome through a scalable experience, then make private access an additional level of support. This gives clients different ways to work with you while protecting your time and creating a stronger revenue model.

  • Create a core group-based program with a defined curriculum and support system.
  • Introduce a premium VIP option with private consultations and more personalized guidance.
  • Price each tier according to the level of access, personalization, and support included.
