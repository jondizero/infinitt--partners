0421
OBSERVATION 01

Your Instagram is splitting attention between 2 conversion paths.

Your Instagram bio sends visitors toward both a webinar and a VSL, giving them two different paths to take instead of one clear next step. Without a clear hierarchy, visitors have to choose their own path, which can create unnecessary friction and make the overall funnel less focused.

  • Visitors may choose the path that feels easiest rather than the one most likely to convert them.
  • Neither funnel gets the full benefit of a clear, consistent entry point.
  • You make it harder to understand which customer journey is actually producing the best results.
0521
after
RECOMMENDATION 01

Build one primary path from attention to conversion

Give visitors a clear route into your funnel, with other assets supporting that journey rather than competing with it.

  • Select one primary entry point based on your audience and conversion data.
  • Align your Instagram bio and calls to action around that path.
  • Use the webinar and VSL in complementary roles where appropriate, rather than presenting them as equal choices.
0621
OBSERVATION 02

Your webinar headline doesn't give people a reason to attend.

Your current headline doesn't clearly communicate what attendees will learn, what they'll walk away with, or why the webinar deserves their time. Interestingly, your thank-you page describes it as the “AI & Robotics Supercycle Portfolio Workshop,” which gives visitors a much clearer idea of the subject and format than the initial headline does.

  • Potential attendees have less information to judge whether the webinar is relevant to them.
  • The value of registering isn't immediately obvious.
  • You may lose qualified registrations before visitors even reach the registration form.
0721
after
RECOMMENDATION 02

Sell the outcome of attending

Make the headline communicate the specific subject, value, and reason the right audience should invest time in the workshop.

  • State what the webinar will help attendees understand or do.
  • Make the AI and robotics investment angle clear upfront.
  • Give visitors a concrete reason to register now, without promising results the workshop can't guarantee.
0821
OBSERVATION 03

You're confirming registrations without building attendance intent.

After registering, visitors receive confirmation, but the page doesn't do much to reinforce why attending the workshop matters. There's no client or student proof to establish credibility, no additional engagement path, and no extra experience designed to build commitment between registration and the event.

  • Registrants have fewer reasons to prioritize the workshop when the time comes.
  • You miss a chance to strengthen trust before the webinar begins.
  • The period between registration and attendance is underused.

after
RECOMMENDATION 03

Turn registration into a commitment-building experience

Use the thank-you page to confirm the booking, reinforce the value of attending, and keep registrants engaged before the event.

  • Add relevant proof, such as genuine client experiences or evidence of your expertise.
  • Give registrants a clear way to join a relevant Telegram or WhatsApp community, if appropriate.
  • Consider a relevant optional offer for qualified registrants without distracting from the main webinar.

Your headline suggests a major shift in wealth creation, but leaves the visitor to guess what that means for them. Without a clearer connection to the audience's interests, the message risks sounding like a broad financial teaser rather than a compelling reason to watch your presentation.

  • The visitor has to work to understand the relevance of the claim.
  • The headline doesn't distinguish your presentation from other investment content.
  • You risk losing attention before the VSL has a chance to build conviction.

RECOMMENDATION 04

Connect the big idea to a specific audience benefit

Keep the broader wealth-shift concept, but make the practical relevance clear enough to motivate the click.

  • Explain what market trend or investment theme you're discussing.
  • State what the viewer can expect to understand or evaluate.

OBSERVATION 05

You're leaving the time between booking and the call underused.

Once a prospect books, the confirmation page does little to maintain their interest or increase their confidence in the decision. The time before the call is an opportunity to build familiarity with your approach and reinforce why the conversation is worth attending, but the current experience doesn't use it.

  • Prospects have fewer opportunities to build confidence before speaking with you.
  • The call begins without the additional context or preparation the page could provide.
  • You miss a chance to strengthen commitment after the prospect has already shown buying intent.
1321
after
RECOMMENDATION 05

Build trust before the sales conversation begins

Create a post-booking experience that continues the sales journey instead of simply acknowledging the appointment.

  • Use a short video to explain the purpose and value of the call.
  • Share relevant case studies or client proof to establish credibility.
  • Set expectations for the conversation and give prospects a clear preparation step.
