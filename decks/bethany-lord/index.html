OBSERVATION 01

Your landing page sends girls straight into a form without selling them first.

The page has no headline or real introduction. The first thing they see is your brand logo and a CTA button. They're presented that without first understanding the value of your offer, your approach, or why they should stay. This creates unnecessary doubt, especially for people who are not familiar with your work.

  • Cold and warm visitors have little context before being asked to apply.
  • You're relying on existing trust instead of building it on the page.
  • Potential clients may abandon the process before you ever get the chance to speak with them.
0518
after
RECOMMENDATION 01

Build desire and trust before asking women to apply.

The current page needs to do some of the selling before the form ever appears. Give visitors a clear reason to pay attention, help them recognize the transformation you're offering, and show them why your approach is worth considering. A short 5–8 minute VSL could make a significant difference here by turning a simple application page into an actual sales experience.

  • Lead with a strong headline that speaks directly to the outcome your audience wants.
  • Use a mini-VSL to explain the problem your audience faces, introduce your method, establish your authority, and show what's possible.
  • Position the application as the next step for women who have understood the value of your program and want help achieving that transformation.
0618
OBSERVATION 02

Your confirmation page isn't moving new leads any closer to becoming customers.

After someone completes your coaching application, the confirmation page doesn't appear to do much to reassure them or strengthen their interest in moving forward. They've just shared personal information and expressed interest in your coaching, but there's no additional experience that makes them feel confident about the decision they've just made.

  • You're missing a chance to reinforce the value of applying.
  • Prospects may lose excitement or become uncertain about what happens next.
  • You're not using this stage to build confidence before the sales conversation.

scroll here
RECOMMENDATION 02

Create a post-application experience that moves serious prospects closer to becoming clients.

Think of the confirmation page as the first stage of your sales process after someone applies. It should make the prospect feel that they've taken an important step toward achieving their goals, while preparing them for what comes next. You can use this space to communicate the process, reinforce your authority, and even introduce additional proof that helps them feel confident about moving forward.

  • Use a thank-you video to acknowledge their application and explain how you evaluate potential coaching clients.
  • Show relevant testimonials and success stories from women who have achieved results through your approach.
  • Give applicants a clear next action, such as scheduling their consultation or preparing for the next stage of the qualification process.

RECOMMENDATION 03

Introduce a VIP transformation program built around a specific outcome.

Create a premium experience for women who don't just want access to a program, but want you to guide them through the transformation more closely. For example, a 12-week coaching program with a personalized strategy and direct support. This gives you something much more valuable to sell than simply another month of membership.

  • Develop a 12-week VIP or 1-on-1 program with a clearly defined transformation and personalized support.
  • Include a higher level of access, accountability, and guidance to justify the premium positioning.
  • Position the monthly membership as a separate option for people who prefer a more accessible, self-directed experience.
