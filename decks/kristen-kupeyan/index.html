＋OBSERVATION 01 SCREENSHOT
OBSERVATION 01

You're sending ready-to-buy prospects to a broken link during your webinar

The webinar asks viewers to go to chatwithdrkupeyan.com before the delayed CTA button appears below the video. The problem is that page that doesn’t exist. A prospect who watches a 40-minute video and clicks the call-to-action is in the 99th percentile of buying intent. Hitting a 404/server error breaks trust instantly, creates frustration, and bleeds 100% of webinar-generated revenue.

  • Fix or replace the broken URL.
  • Align the webinar's spoken instructions with the actual working CTA button.
  • Make the booking action accessible when the offer is introduced.

RECOMMENDATION 01

Make the transition from webinar to booking completely seamless.

Every CTA should take viewers directly to the same working booking experience. The goal is to remove technical friction so interested prospects can act immediately when they're convinced.

  • Use one consistent booking URL across the webinar, slides, and page.
  • Test the complete journey from registration through booking.
  • Ensure the CTA is visible and accessible at the moment viewers are instructed to take action.

＋OBSERVATION 02 SCREENSHOT
OBSERVATION 02

Your application form doesn't qualify prospects financially

A strong application should help you understand more than a prospect's health goals. It should also establish whether she can afford your service so you don’t waste your time with bad leads.

  • You're potentially spending time with people who cannot afford the service.
  • Your team has no clear indication of a prospect's investment readiness.
  • Qualified and unqualified applicants enter the same sales process.

RECOMMENDATION 02

Introduce at least one financial qualification question

Your application should set expectations around the nature and investment level of your programs. This helps prospects make an informed decision before booking and allows your team to prioritize conversations with people who are genuinely prepared to move forward.

  • Add a clear statement explaining that your programs are private-pay and do not bill insurance directly.
  • Ask whether the prospect is prepared to make a private investment in her health if accepted.
  • Use the answer to guide lead qualification and determine the appropriate next step.

OBSERVATION 03

Your content isn't generating enough traffic for your offers

Your Instagram, YouTube, and Facebook presence isn't generating enough traffic to consistently introduce new prospects to your Skool/Webinar offers. Your webinar can convert interested prospects, but it still needs a consistent flow of new registrations. If your content isn't reaching enough people, the webinar doesn't have enough opportunities to generate new clients.

  • Too few new people are entering the funnel.
  • Your webinar's conversion potential is limited by the volume of registrations.
  • You're missing opportunities to build demand before prospects are actively looking for a solution.

RECOMMENDATION 03

Invest into a content engine that turns your expertise into traffic

Build a content system where one strong educational idea can reach people in multiple formats and across multiple platforms. This increases the opportunities for potential clients to discover you without requiring you to create every piece of content from scratch.

  • Use long-form YouTube videos to explain complex topics and demonstrate your expertise.
  • Repurpose those insights into short-form videos, posts, and educational content for Instagram and Facebook.
  • Create clear content-to-webinar pathways so attention has somewhere to go.

＋OBSERVATION 04 SCREENSHOT
OBSERVATION 04

Your on-demand webinar doesn't create enough commitment or anticipation

Your current webinar is available immediately after someone registers. That makes it convenient, but it also makes the experience feel similar to watching any other YouTube video. When someone registers and can watch immediately, the webinar becomes another piece of on-demand content. There's no shared experience, no scheduled commitment, and no structured lead-up that makes the presentation feel like an important opportunity.

  • Registrants have little incentive to prioritize watching now.
  • You're not creating anticipation between registration and attendance.
  • The webinar has less perceived importance than a dedicated, set event.
1117
＋RECOMMENDATION 04 IMAGE
after
RECOMMENDATION 04

Give the webinar a weekly schedule and build a pre-event experience around it.

Instead of giving every registrant immediate access, establish a consistent weekly webinar time. This creates a defined event that people can plan around and gives you a window to build engagement before the presentation.

  • Set a recurring weekly date and time so registrants know exactly when to attend.
  • Create a pre-webinar email sequence that builds anticipation, introduces key ideas, and reminds people to show up.
  • Use the event to present your methodology, answer common questions, and guide qualified attendees toward a consultation.
