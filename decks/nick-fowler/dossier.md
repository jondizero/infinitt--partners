# Nick Fowler — Funnel Audit Dossier

## Research snapshot
- Nick Fowler positions himself as a TikTok Shop expert / creator mentor.
- His public site says he has helped generate over $10M in sales through affiliate content in 2024 and teaches aspiring creators without requiring a huge following.
- Affiliate Formula currently leads with a mentorship offer, with a $10k+/month framing and 5–10 hours/week language.
- The public funnel includes application/qualification, testimonials, team credibility, FAQs and direct mentorship access.
- The current funnel explicitly says people with full-time jobs can start with 30 minutes/day, while noting stronger results among people who can dedicate more time.
- No dedicated webinar, VSL, or low-ticket front-end was identified in the public pages reviewed; the visible ecosystem is primarily mentorship/course/community/direct-response content.

## Selected bottlenecks
1. Offer presentation does not communicate the full value immediately.
2. Booking confirmation / thank-you page is not being used to build momentum.
3. Post-booking email sequence relies mainly on reminders rather than deliberate indoctrination.

## Opportunities
1. Create more ways to work with Nick: Webinar + VSL + Low Ticket before premium mentorship.
2. Expand content distribution beyond the current platform footprint, with X as a deliberate authority/discovery channel.

## Awareness
Nick already sells TikTok Shop education/mentorship. The opportunity is not to educate the market that the category exists, but to create more acquisition mechanisms around an existing expertise and premium offer.

## Research limitations
Screenshot-specific observations use the screenshots supplied for this audit. Automated access to some social platforms can be restricted, so social profiles were not treated as exhaustive funnel evidence.
