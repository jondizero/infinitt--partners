OBSERVATION 01

Interest doesn't have enough room to develop.

I went through the early-access journey myself, and right now the experience moves almost immediately from interest to a form. That makes it harder for someone to understand what they're getting, why it matters and why they should want to be part of it.

  • Limited opportunity to communicate the value.
  • The offer isn't fully explained before the commitment.
  • Interest has to turn into action too quickly.

after
RECOMMENDATION 01

Give the offer room to sell itself.

Create a dedicated experience where prospects can understand the vision, value and outcome before deciding whether they want to take the next step.

  • Build the offer around a clear value proposition.
  • Create desire before asking for commitment.
  • Give prospects a reason to join before they submit.

OBSERVATION 02

The relationship stops right after the signup.

After submitting the form, there was no next step or additional experience. That's a missed opportunity to keep people engaged while their interest is still fresh.

  • No reinforcement after joining.
  • No opportunity to build anticipation.
  • Interest isn't being developed after the signup.

after
RECOMMENDATION 02

Keep building the relationship after they join.

Use the moment immediately after signup to reinforce the decision, build anticipation and give people a reason to stay engaged until the offer is ready.

  • Confirm that they made the right decision.
  • Tackle common objections they're probably thinking of.

OBSERVATION 03

Early interest is being left to cool off.

Someone signing up for early access is a clear signal of interest. Without consistent communication afterwards, that interest can naturally fade before the offer is even ready.

  • No reason to stay engaged.
  • Interest can fade between signup and launch.
  • Launching starts from a colder audience.

after
RECOMMENDATION 03

Keep interest warm.

Use the time before launch to continuously strengthen the relationship, making prospects more familiar with the business and more invested in what's coming.

  • Stay top of mind.
