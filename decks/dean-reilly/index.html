After going through the booking process, I noticed the journey ends immediately after the call is scheduled. Once someone books a call, they're actively evaluating whether they've made the right decision. Without reinforcing that choice, the business misses one of its best opportunities to build trust before the sales conversation.

  • Confidence isn't actively developed
