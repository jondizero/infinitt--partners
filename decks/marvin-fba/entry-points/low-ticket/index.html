Frequently Asked Questions

Will these scripts guarantee supplier approval?

No script can guarantee approval. Suppliers set their own requirements, and applications are evaluated individually.

Do I need my own product?

Amazon wholesale typically focuses on reselling products from established brands through authorized supply relationships, rather than creating a product from scratch.

Should I buy inventory right away?

No. Understand supplier requirements, product restrictions, and the full economics before committing capital.
