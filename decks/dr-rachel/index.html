OBSERVATION 01

Your IG bio is promoting an expired event

Your event page still promotes a past event, displays a countdown at zero, and asks visitors to secure their spot. Anyone clicking through is being sent to an offer that no longer exists. That creates unnecessary confusion at the point where someone is deciding whether to take the next step.

  • Visitors may leave when they realize the event is no longer current.
  • The outdated page can make your business appear inactive or poorly maintained.
  • You're missing the opportunity to redirect existing traffic toward your next event or offer.

RECOMMENDATION 01

Give the visitor a relevant next step

Don't let an old event page become a dead end. Replace the expired promotion with a clear next step that captures interest and moves people toward your current offer.

  • Update the page with your next event date and a working registration CTA.
  • If the next date isn't confirmed, offer a waitlist or email notification.
  • Update your Instagram bio and older promotional links so they direct visitors to a current opportunity

OBSERVATION 03

Your Confirmation page confirms the booking, but doesn’t build intention to showing up

Once someone books a call, your confirmation page gives you a valuable window to strengthen their commitment before the meeting. Right now, that window is underused: there’s no clear calendar-add button or focused video sequence to reinforce why the call matters and address common concerns. The result? Prospects can forget why they booked, let doubts creep in, or simply fail to show up. Every no-show wastes time that could have been spent with a qualified buyer.

  • Make it easier for prospects to remember and protect the appointment.
  • Address doubts before they become reasons to cancel or skip the call.
  • Help prospects arrive better informed and more ready for the conversation.

RECOMMENDATION 03

Turn the confirmation page into a show-up system

Give people a clear next step and use targeted content to build confidence before the conversation.

  • Explain what the call will cover and how to prepare.
  • Add short videos addressing the biggest questions and objections prospects typically have.
  • Connect the confirmation page to a coordinated reminder sequence so the message continues after they leave the page.
