Expertise is no longer what separates the winners

More creators launch courses. More coaches enter the market. More money flows into education every year. But as the market matures, the winners aren't simply the ones with the best offer. WHERE GROWTH ACTUALLY LEAKS

Growth isn't usually lost at the top of the funnel

It's everything that happens after someone clicks. The slow funnels. Poor follow-up. Weak onboarding. Disconnected automations. Prospects who lose momentum before booking. Each one feels small. Together, they quietly become one of the largest costs in the business.

OBSERVATION 01

You're giving people too many places to go.

Your Instagram bio has too many links competing for attention. Instead of making it obvious what someone should do next, you're asking visitors to choose between several options before they've even entered your funnel.

  • Some visitors may get overwhelmed and click nothing.
  • Traffic gets split across different destinations instead of going toward one main goal.
  • Potential leads can leave your profile without taking any meaningful action.

RECOMMENDATION 01

Give visitors one clear next step

Make the main action obvious, and let secondary links support it rather than compete with it.

  • Choose the offer or funnel you most want to drive traffic toward.
  • Make that the primary link in your bio.
  • Keep other links available where they make sense, without distracting from the main action.

OBSERVATION 02

The headlines blend into the category

Everything is too broad. It could describe almost any business or investing program. Without a more precise message, the headline doesn't give prospective students a strong reason to believe your courses are the best fit for their goals.

  • Your offer risks blending in with generic real estate education.
  • The course's specific curriculum and practical value aren't doing enough work upfront.
  • Visitors may compare programs mainly on price or familiarity instead of what they'll learn.

RECOMMENDATION 02

Speak directly to the buyer

Make the headline specific enough that the right prospect immediately recognizes the problem or opportunity the course addresses.

OBSERVATION 03

Your offer's full value isn't obvious

Your page mentions mentorship, weekly training, community, business support, courses, and tools, but the value of those inclusions isn't tied to a clear set of deliverables or outcomes. Visitors can see that the program includes a lot, but they still have to work out how those pieces will help their own investing business.

  • Prospects may struggle to understand what makes the program worth investing in.
  • The sales call has to do more work explaining the offer from scratch.
  • People who need a clearer picture of the value may leave before booking.

RECOMMENDATION 03

Make the value of the program tangible

Show prospects what they'll actually receive and how those pieces support their real estate investing goals.

OBSERVATION 04

Your checkout misses an opportunity to grow each order

Your checkout page doesn't clearly break down what buyers receive, and it doesn't offer a relevant add-on or next step. At the moment when someone is deciding whether to pay, the page could do more to remind them what they're getting and help interested buyers access complementary resources.

  • Buyers have less information to reinforce their decision at checkout.
  • You miss an opportunity to increase average order value through relevant add-ons.
  • The business earns only the core product revenue from customers who might be willing to purchase more.

RECOMMENDATION 04

Make checkout work harder for every sale

Use the checkout to reinforce the core offer and introduce relevant complementary purchases without distracting from the main transaction.
