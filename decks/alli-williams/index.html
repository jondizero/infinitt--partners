OBSERVATION 01

You're confirming registrations, but not preparing people to show up to your webinar

After someone registers for your webinar, the confirmation page is a valuable opportunity to reinforce their decision and build excitement. If the page simply confirms the registration without a thank-you video, calendar reminder, or meaningful proof, you're missing the chance to make attending feel important.

  • Registrants may forget the event or fail to prioritize it.
  • You miss an opportunity to reinforce why they signed up in the first place.
  • Prospects may arrive with less confidence and interest than they had when they registered.
0522
scroll here
RECOMMENDATION 01

Turn the confirmation page into the first step of the webinar experience.

Don't treat registration as the finish line. Use that moment to strengthen the prospect's commitment and make them look forward to attending.

  • Build excitement around what they'll learn and why it's worth their time.
  • Reinforce the decision with credibility and proof from people who've benefited from your approach.
  • Make attending feel like a natural next step rather than something they simply need to remember.
0622
OBSERVATION 02

Your coaching offer has no real sales experience around it.

A direct Google Form doesn't give prospects much about what they’re applying for. There's no dedicated experience explaining the program, who it's designed for, or why someone should consider joining. For a prospect who doesn't already know you or understand your coaching, filling out an application is a significant ask. They need to understand what they're applying for and why it's worth their time first.

  • Prospects may abandon the form because they don't have enough context to commit.
  • You're missing an opportunity to communicate the value and differentiation of your coaching.
  • The application process may attract fewer qualified prospects than it could.

RECOMMENDATION 02

Give your coaching a proper buying journey.

Your coaching deserves to be presented as an actual offer, not just an application link. Build enough understanding and desire before asking prospects to raise their hand.

  • Make the coaching experience and its value easier to understand.
  • Establish why the program is different from other solutions prospects may be considering.
  • Create a natural transition from learning about the offer to applying for it.

Your webinar page doesn't give visitors a concrete reason to prioritize this class

Your webinar page communicates the overall concept, but it doesn't immediately give visitors a concrete reason to prioritize this particular class. Your philosophy is there, but the headline could do more to make the outcome feel specific and desirable.

  • Your message may resonate broadly without feeling deeply relevant to a specific prospect.
  • Visitors may struggle to see exactly how the class relates to their situation.
  • You risk attracting registrations from people who aren't necessarily the best fit for your coaching offer.

Your Instagram link in bio is giving people too many directions

Your Instagram bio is one of the first places interested prospects go to learn more about your business. But when it's filled with multiple links and competing calls to action, people have to figure out which one matters most. Every additional choice creates another opportunity for someone to leave without taking action.

  • Interested visitors can get distracted by secondary links.
  • Your main offer receives less attention than it deserves.
  • You're potentially losing leads before they even enter your funnel.
